How JalCycle Works

1. Tap when water comes Whenever municipal or tanker water arrives at your home, open JalCycle and tap "Water Came Today".
2. Pick expected gap Choose your usual cycle (4, 5, 6, or 7 days). JalCycle auto-recommends your household's most frequent pattern.
3. Instant clear predictions Opening the app instantly shows the exact expected arrival date and days left, with zero internet or logins required.
4. Sync with family Use the WhatsApp Share button in Settings to send your updated water log to family members so everyone stays synced.
